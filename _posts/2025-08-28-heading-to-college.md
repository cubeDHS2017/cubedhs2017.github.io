Today I left for college. We left just after 4 in the morning. Now that I have finished packing and have nothing else to do till I get to college the stress is off and not it is just a waiting game till I get there. 

I am excited to get there to start this new chapter of my life. I know that I am ready but at the same time it just feels really weird to no longer be a kid. Being an adult is something that I thought would be fun but now that it is here it is a lot of responsibility.

I am going to be majoring in Business and Information Technology. I am excited to studying this because I have always enjoyed tinkering and messing around with computers. The business is also interesting to me because it should equip me to help others better as an IT professional. It also will be helpful if I end up starting my own business or startup in the future. 

As far as the classes I am taking this semester, I am going to be taking a required introductory computer applications class. I did not want to have to take this class but there was no easy way for me to drop the prerequisite or postpone it. Then I am taking a marketing class. I am very excited for my networking class that I am taking hoping that it will help me better setup and run my homelab network infrastructure. I am also taking Old Testament survey. Finally, I am taking a modern world history class. So overall the classes this semester are not going to be crazy hard but it is defiantly going to feel like a lot of work since it is going to be my first semester with a full college workload.

On top of those 15 credit hours I am also going to be taking private piano lessons and also participating in a choir. While this is not a class, I am hopefully going to be working at the IT help desk. I know this will help me get a lot of very practical IT experience and also put the things I am learning into practice. It should also help me to get a better understanding of what field in IT I want to pursue.

I know that this first semester is going to be a growing experience. Not just academically but also as a person. I am excited to start this journey tomorrow. If you would like to follow along with my journey follow my RSS feed.
